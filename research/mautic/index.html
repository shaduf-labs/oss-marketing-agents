Platform dossier · updated 26 Sep 2026

Mautic: marketing operations require an operator.

Mautic is the platform-layer option for self-hosted contact, form, segment, campaign, and email operations. It is conditional on operating the database, transport, scheduler or workers, credentials, access, monitoring, and recovery.

Current auditThe 26 September document audit covers Mautic Community 7.2.1 source and documentation. It did not install Mautic or run a contact, send, worker, cron job, tracking, webhook, API, or external action.

What the checked sources support

Declared source boundary.
The 7.2.1 composer metadata declares GPL-3.0. That applies to the checked repository package, not every separately installed plugin, transport, provider, queue, CRM, or hosted service.

Documented application surface.
7.2 documentation covers forms, contacts, preferences/DNC, segments, campaigns, transport, queues, cron, webhooks, roles, and reports. A documented feature is not evidence of a live deployment or a policy enforcement result.

Campaign execution boundary

Campaign membership and events require the documented campaign cron commands. If an email queue is configured, a consumer must process it. Mail transport configuration also needs a provider and sender. A campaign UI, DNC field, queue setting, role, or active switch does not establish that delivery is approved, compliant, deliverable, monitored, or recoverable.

The documentation describes granular roles and DNC/preference controls, but this audit did not establish immutable consent, all-channel suppression, a two-person approval gate, least privilege in a deployment, or a live-send result.

Conditional decision

Choose it whenYou need owned marketing-operation state and can assign operators for infrastructure, transport/DNS, scheduling, credentials, data governance, monitoring, and tested recovery. Do not choose it for zero-operations drafting or unattended outbound marketing.

Safe first evaluation: use an isolated local environment and fictional data only. Do not configure production credentials, real transport, external actions, tracking, public webhooks, or unattended cron/workers. Keep any form or campaign non-public and inactive; stop before an external call.

Maintenance and open evidence

The 7.2.1 release record was labelled Latest at the audit date. The security page documents a reporting scope and route. These are maintenance signals, not proof that a selected deployment is supported, patched, secure, or ready for production.

Open evidence includes the current 7.2 runtime/database matrix, retention and audit-log semantics, consent and suppression behavior across systems, approval controls, secret management, connector scopes, reconciliation/rollback, deliverability/performance, and real deployment backup/restore status.
